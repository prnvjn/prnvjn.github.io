1. **Artifact title** — a clear, descriptive title.
2. **Introduction** — context for the work.
3. **Artifact description** — objective, process, individual or group contribution, and tools and technologies.
4. **Artifact-specific value proposition** — unique value and relevance to your overall proposition and audience.
5. **Customization for audience** — adaptations and their relevance.
6. **Reflection** — significance, demonstrated competencies, and lessons learned.
7. **Feedback and revisions** — feedback received, changes made, and their impact.
8. **References** — resources consulted or cited.

Include the actual artifact file, image, or link alongside its write-up. Do not publish the example from the supplied document as personal work.
