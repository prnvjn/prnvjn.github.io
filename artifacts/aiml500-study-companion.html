Introduction

I built the AIML-500 Study Companion for my Workshop 1 AI Lab. It is designed to help university students practice AI/ML concepts through mock interviews.

Artifact Description

My role: Sole creator. Tool: Mizou.

I configured the tutor’s instructions, welcome message, and rules. The goal is to support exam preparation using clear questions, feedback, and follow-ups. Responses are instructed to stay within 500 characters.

Artifact-Specific Value Proposition

This artifact shows my ability to shape an AI tool around a learning goal. I chose it to demonstrate practical prompt design to employers and collaborators interested in applied AI.

Customization for Audience

The chatbot is tailored to AIML-500 university students through its course focus, mock-interview format, simple language, and supportive tone. These choices are intended to make practice easier to follow.

Reflection

Building it independently demonstrates my ability to turn an idea into a working chatbot. The Mizou tier I used did not let me add a course knowledge base, highlighting the importance of matching a tool’s capabilities to the task.

Feedback and Revisions

I developed the initial study-companion idea into a mock-interview format with short responses and follow-up questions. Test results and learner feedback are not yet documented.
